## Confirmed root causes and corrections

1. N078's traffic and growth came from independent generators. Growth now derives from comparable canonical traffic records: (3549/3471 − 1) × 100 = 2.247191011…%, displayed 2.25%. Canonical observations, score components, site cases and server scenarios share this definition. Missing, zero base, stale/conflicting and different source families produce explicit unavailable states. No forecast/proxy silently substitutes for measured synthetic growth. Existing frozen evidence retains the historical definition/value; model version 0.3 identifies new scoring.
2. Demo rules had a generic service-rule presentation. Legal obligations now have a separate sourced register and applicability/measurement gates. The 98%/five-hour defaults remain demonstration settings; the legacy legal/fine utility functions explicitly throw disabled errors. No automatic penalty or benefit from avoided fines is calculated.
3. Screening eligibility relied on site metrics without causal diagnosis. Candidates now expose screening, diagnosis sufficiency, engineering state and review readiness separately. Confirmed cause references and engineering review are required for technically supported corrective options; missing evidence stays visible.
4. Allocation used priority points per dollar, favouring cheap maintenance. Expenditure/count summaries, leading omission reasons and same-evidence Balanced/Growth/Reliability comparison explain the method. Service Assurance requires a reviewer-confirmed cell finding, current matching obligation interpretation, cause-specific engineering/regulatory review and supported option. Sharing also requires commercial/approval evidence. Supported selected costs enter allocation instead of fixture costs. Explicit must-fund entries require a reviewer rationale.
5. Scenario wording conflicted with execution. Balanced uses persisted active weights; other objectives use named presets. Unsaved study weights remain local. Saved policy, legal/applicability and corrective-case snapshots stay frozen. Supported greedy engine 0.3/0.4 replay uses saved candidates/configuration; unsupported engines are reported rather than silently replayed under defaults.
6. Maps depended on external OSM tiles and retained a large canvas after an error. Loading timeout/error status now collapses the failed canvas and offers retry plus a regional, keyboard-accessible filtered site fallback. No map token is required by this raster source; provider/network/browser availability remains external.
7. Raw floating point values and unbounded lists harmed review. Shared number/percent/hour formatting keeps full internal precision; investment/evidence/case searches and progressive disclosure reduce initial content. Hashes/provenance are expandable. New workflow tabs and inputs have explicit readable styling and focus treatment.

## Workflow and business case

The Service assurance screen covers the obligation register, independent synthetic cell counters, frozen findings, diagnosis, build/upgrade/share/maintain/defer options, engineering and regulatory review, explicit Finance submission, advisory investment review, external approval reference, implementation tracking and independent post-intervention outcome evidence. No submissions or approvals are automatic. Case inputs freeze at submission; later implementation events append to the retained timeline. Cases retain incident snapshots and exact source/rule interpretations. Known selected cost, defer alternative and a measurement plan are required for review readiness; benefits may remain unknown.

Business-case assumptions distinguish cost, incremental contribution low/base/high, OPEX savings, recurring costs, implementation period/horizon, source/reviewer, operational improvement and defer impact. Only complete compatible reviewed assumptions yield an illustrative undiscounted net cash-flow range. Site contribution is not incremental benefit; included failure costs are not added to savings automatically. ROI, NPV and causally proven benefits are not claimed.

Sharing captures infrastructure/counterpart/capacity evidence, feasibility, setup/recurring estimates, security/access, negotiation status, agreement/approval references, owner and conditional response deadline. Unknown feasibility remains unknown; a draft agreement is not an approval.
