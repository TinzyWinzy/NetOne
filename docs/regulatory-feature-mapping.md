# Regulatory sources and feature mapping — 5 October 2026

This is a source-verification record for a synthetic pilot, not a consolidated legal opinion or compliance certificate. Gazette copies were retrieved, extracted and the SI 154 cellular KPI table visually inspected. The register separates source retrieval, legal interpretation review, applicability and individual finding review. Demonstration rules cannot be relabelled legal through the API.

The register retains instrument/jurisdiction, exact reference, source reference/hash where retrieved, version/lineage, dates, entity/service/technology/scope, KPI numerator/denominator/unit/comparator/window, conditions/exceptions, reporting/correction, evidence, owner and reviewer/date. Source hashes identify the retrieved gazetted copy, not a guarantee of current legal status. Dates shown for statutory instruments are their Gazette publication dates; legal owners must confirm commencement and current applicability. The principal, sharing and data-protection texts are documentary register entries rather than automatic numerical evaluators.

## Missing documents and unverified obligations

- Actual NetOne licence, schedules/conditions, amendments and current service/spectrum scope.
- Applicable POTRAZ directions, warning/rectification notices, exemptions, extensions and approved reporting formats.
- Authoritative assurance of subsequent amendments/current consolidation. Research found the cited principal and amendments; absence of a further search result does not prove there are no later instruments.
- Approved cell KPI measurement/sampling/probing protocol, reporting periods and data reliability/integrity treatment. 4G speed and reliability/integrity parameters are present in SI 154 but are not automated by the ratio evaluator in this milestone.
- Infrastructure sharing agreements, Authority approval references, reservation evidence, request event and actual public-holiday calendar.
- Controller/processor assignment, applicable licence/exemption, DPO appointment/certification, processing agreements and deployment/cross-border review.

These gaps are visible as unassessed applicability, under-review interpretation, unknown data or disabled evaluation. A reviewer-confirmed synthetic finding is not an imposed regulatory finding. No app action sends a regulatory report or approves capital.
